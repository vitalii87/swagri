# Code of conduct

Swagri welcomes respectful technical disagreement and evidence that challenges
the project's assumptions.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- give actionable, specific feedback;
- respect privacy and security boundaries;
- credit prior work and collaborators;
- make space for contributors with different experience levels;
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as harassment or abuse.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of
private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner.
